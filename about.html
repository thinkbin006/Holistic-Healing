Welcome. I'm Ruchi.

My practice brings together Reiki, spiritual healing, numerology, meditation facilitation, NLP and supportive conversations. I also offer Tarot reflection and therapeutic yoga and meditation enquiries.

Since 2019, I have been exploring ways to support personal reflection and a sense of connection through these different practices. What matters to me is a personal approach: listening to what you would like to explore, explaining the practice and making room for your choices.

You might arrive with a specific interest, a question, or simply the wish to pause. You do not need to have everything worked out before getting in touch.

Your pace. Your choice.

Each person brings a different background and experience. We can discuss the scope and suitability of a session before arranging it. You are welcome to ask about my training, how an offering works and what it does not provide.

Spiritual and complementary wellbeing practices do not replace medical or mental-health care. I do not promise a particular outcome, and your decisions remain your own.
