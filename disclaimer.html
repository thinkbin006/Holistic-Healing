Clear expectations

Understanding the scope.

A thoughtful practice starts with clarity about its purpose, boundaries and limitations.

Complementary wellbeing, not medical treatment

The offerings described here are intended for personal reflection, spiritual exploration and complementary wellbeing. They do not provide medical diagnosis, psychotherapy or emergency support, and should not replace appropriate professional care.

Do not stop or change prescribed treatment because of a spiritual practice or reading. Discuss health concerns with an appropriately qualified medical or mental-health professional.

Spiritual and reflective frameworks

Reiki, spiritual healing and chakra practices draw on spiritual traditions. Descriptions of energy or chakras refer to those traditions, not established medical mechanisms. Numerology and Tarot are interpretive practices, not scientific prediction or medical, financial or legal advice.

Individual experiences and personal choice

Experiences vary, and no specific physical, emotional or life outcome is guaranteed. A session intention is not a promise of results. You are free to ask questions, decline an exercise or decide that an offering is not right for you.

Counselling, yoga and suitability

Counselling enquiries here concern supportive wellbeing conversations, not diagnosis, psychotherapy or a regulated clinical service. Ask Ruchi about her training and scope before arranging a session. Therapeutic yoga enquiries should include a discussion of the approach and your suitability; consult your treating professional when health conditions, injuries or pregnancy may affect participation.

Enquiries and appointments

Opening WhatsApp, calling or sending an email does not automatically book an appointment. Confirm the format, duration, fee, availability and cancellation arrangements directly before committing to a session. No payments are collected on this website.

Urgent help

This website and its contact channels are not emergency or crisis services. If you need immediate help, contact local emergency services or an appropriate qualified professional.
