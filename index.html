Ask a different question ↗
I am new to this. Where should I start?+

You do not need to know which practice to choose. Read the service introductions, or send Ruchi a short message about what you would like to explore. You can discuss an approach before deciding whether to arrange a session.

Are sessions online or in person?+

Ruchi is based in Indira Nagar, Ujjain. Please ask about the available format for your chosen service before making plans. An enquiry does not confirm an appointment or a walk-in visit.

How much does a session cost, and how long does it last?+

Ask Ruchi for the fee, duration and availability for the service you are interested in. Agree these details, and any cancellation arrangements, before confirming your session.

Do I need previous experience or a particular belief?+

No previous experience is needed to ask about a practice. Some offerings use spiritual traditions, while others focus on reflection or guided attention. Discuss what feels comfortable for you; you can choose not to take part in any practice.

How should I prepare?+

Choose comfortable clothing, keep water nearby and allow yourself a quiet, undisturbed space. A chair is a welcome alternative to sitting on the floor. Incense and other rituals are optional. Ask about any preparation specific to your session.

Can these services replace medical or mental-health care?+

No. These are complementary wellbeing and spiritual practices, not medical diagnosis, psychotherapy or emergency support. Continue appropriate professional care and discuss any health concerns with a qualified professional. Individual experiences vary.
