Your information

Privacy, in plain language.

What this website does, what it does not collect, and what happens when you choose to get in touch.

Browsing this website

This version of the website does not use enquiry forms, client accounts, payments, analytics trackers or marketing cookies. It does not ask you to upload documents or maintain a client profile. Images and other site assets are served with the website rather than through embedded social feeds.

Choosing a contact option

WhatsApp links open a draft message that you can review and send yourself. Telephone and email links open the relevant app on your device. Instagram links take you to Instagram. The website does not send these messages automatically or store their contents.

When you use those services, their own privacy practices apply. Any information you choose to share directly with Ruchi is part of that separate communication, not a submission to this website.

Share only what is needed

Your name or a brief introduction and the offering you are interested in are enough for an initial enquiry. Please avoid sending medical records, diagnoses, identity documents or sensitive personal histories at this stage.

Hosting and technical information

A hosting provider may process technical connection information such as IP addresses and access logs. Production hosting has not yet been selected for this local version. Provider-specific details and any changes in data collection must be reviewed before public launch.

Questions about privacy

For questions about information you have shared directly with Ruchi, contact meetuujjain@gmail.com. Please ask about the handling of sensitive information before sharing it.
