About this practice

Chakra Balancing draws on traditions that describe spiritual energy centres called chakras. You can approach this framework as a way to reflect on your inner life, with themes ranging from a sense of belonging to communication and connection. Curiosity is enough; there is no ideal state you need to demonstrate.

What to expect

The familiar seven-chakra framework offers different themes for exploration. Root chakra symbolism relates to grounding and security, while heart and throat symbolism can invite reflection on compassion and expression. These associations belong to spiritual tradition, rather than established anatomy or measurements of health.

Ruchi's portfolio describes meditation, visualisation and Reiki among approaches used in chakra work. Ask which practices she proposes for your session and what each would involve. You can discuss a theme that feels relevant instead of treating every part of the framework as a problem to correct.

For example, reflecting on expression might mean noticing when it is easy or difficult to say what you need. Any interpretation is an invitation to consider your experience, not a label that defines you. You can question it, set it aside or ask to pause.

Preparing for your session

Bring a simple intention, such as exploring connection or making room for reflection. Choose comfortable clothing and discuss suitable seating; sitting on the floor is not a requirement.

No crystals, incense or special ritual are needed to begin a conversation about this service. Ask Ruchi about the session format, duration and fee before arranging a session.

Before you begin

A little more clarity.

Do I need to know the names of the chakras?+

No prior knowledge is needed to enquire. Ask Ruchi to explain the traditional ideas in everyday language and discuss which themes interest you.

Does a chakra interpretation identify a medical problem?+

No. A chakra interpretation cannot assess organs or establish a diagnosis. Discuss physical or mental-health concerns with an appropriate healthcare professional.
