About this practice

How you describe a situation can shape the questions you ask about it. NLP, short for Neuro-Linguistic Programming, offers a setting to examine communication and personal goals. You might be interested in expressing a need more clearly, looking at an assumption, or turning a broad intention into something easier to discuss.

What to expect

The focus is on everyday language and conscious reflection. Bring a situation you would like to understand, then ask Ruchi which communication or goal-reflection exercises would be appropriate. There is no need to arrive with a polished plan.

For example, "I want to communicate better" could become a more specific intention: "I would like to explain my availability clearly." You could consider what you want to say, what you are assuming about the conversation, and which parts are within your control.

Looking at another perspective does not mean dismissing a real difficulty or forcing a positive interpretation. You can recognise practical constraints while exploring different words or possible next steps. These exercises do not promise to reprogramme the subconscious, change other people or treat a mental-health condition.

Preparing for your session

Think of one ordinary situation and what you would like to explore about it. You can keep names and identifying details private. Optional notes may help you remember the question that matters most.

Consider what a small, realistic step might look like rather than expecting an instant transformation. Ask Ruchi about the session format, duration and fee before arranging a session.

Before you begin

A little more clarity.

What kind of goal can I bring?+

An everyday communication or personal-development goal is a useful starting point, such as expressing a request clearly or deciding what a manageable next step could be.

Will NLP change my thoughts automatically?+

No automatic change or particular result is promised. The emphasis is on consciously considering language, perspectives and actions you may choose to practise.
