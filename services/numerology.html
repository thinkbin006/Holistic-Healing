Numerology

Numerology is the ancient study of numbers and their energetic influence on our lives. By examining the numbers derived from your birth date and name, we can uncover valuable insights about your personality, life purpose, challenges, and opportunities.

How Numerology Works

Every letter has a numerical value, and every number has a vibrational quality. By calculating your core numbers - Life Path, Expression, Soul Urge, Personality, and Birthday numbers - we can map out your unique energetic blueprint.

Benefits of Numerology

  • Gain deeper self-awareness and understanding of your true nature
  • Discover your life purpose and natural talents
  • Understand relationship dynamics and compatibility
  • Identify personal challenges and how to overcome them
  • Make better decisions aligned with your life path
  • Recognize timing cycles and opportunities

What to Expect in a Session

During our 60-minute session, we'll explore your core numbers and their meanings. I'll provide a detailed analysis of your numerological chart and explain how these energies manifest in different areas of your life. You'll receive practical guidance on how to align with your true path and overcome challenges revealed by your numbers.

After our session, you'll receive a comprehensive written report summarizing our discussion and key insights for future reference.
