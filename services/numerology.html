About this practice

Sometimes a different starting point can help you put familiar questions into words. Numerology uses the symbolism of numbers associated with a name or date of birth to open a reflective conversation. You might be interested in exploring priorities, personal interests or how you describe a new chapter in life.

What to expect

Ruchi can discuss number-based interpretations and the themes you wish to consider. These meanings come from numerological traditions; they are not scientifically established facts about your personality, health or future.

Treat an interpretation as a question rather than a verdict. If a theme such as independence arises, you could consider where you value autonomy and where you welcome support. If an interpretation does not resonate, there is no need to make your experience fit it.

The useful work is in your own reflection: what feels relevant, what needs more thought, and what practical information would help you consider a choice. Numbers cannot guarantee compatibility, career success or an outcome. Your decisions remain yours, with room to change your mind as circumstances change.

Preparing for your session

Choose one or two open questions rather than asking for a certain prediction. "What priorities would I like to explore?" leaves more room for reflection than a demand for a fixed answer.

Ask Ruchi which personal details are necessary before sharing them. Keep an initial enquiry brief and do not send identity documents or sensitive histories. Ask Ruchi about the session format, duration and fee before arranging a session.
