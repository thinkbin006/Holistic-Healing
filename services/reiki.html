Reiki Healing

Reiki is a gentle yet powerful Japanese energy healing technique that promotes deep relaxation, reduces stress, and supports the body's natural healing abilities. Meaning "universal life energy," Reiki works by channeling healing energy through the practitioner's hands to the recipient.

How Reiki Works

During a Reiki session, I act as a conduit for universal life force energy, directing it to areas of your body and energy field that need healing. The energy flows where it is most needed, addressing physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual imbalances.

Benefits of Reiki

  • Deep relaxation and stress reduction
  • Accelerated physical healing
  • Release of emotional blockages and past trauma
  • Improved mental clarity and focus
  • Enhanced spiritual connection and intuition
  • Balanced energy and overall well-being

What to Expect in a Session

During our 60-minute session, you'll lie fully clothed on a massage table while I place my hands lightly on or above various parts of your body. You may experience sensations of warmth, tingling, or deep relaxation as the energy flows. Many clients enter a meditative state or fall asleep during treatment.

After the session, we'll discuss any insights that came through and I'll provide guidance for integrating the healing energy.
