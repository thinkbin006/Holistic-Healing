About this practice

Reiki offers an invitation to step away from everyday demands and spend time with your own experience. At Holistic Healing with Ruchi Gandhi, you can explore whether this spiritual practice has a place in your personal wellbeing routine, without an expectation to feel or achieve something particular.

What to expect

Reiki is a Japanese spiritual tradition whose language includes the idea of universal life energy. This is a traditional belief, not an established scientific explanation of how the body works.

You might come with an intention such as making room for stillness, reflecting on a busy period, or exploring your spiritual interests. Your intention need not be a problem to solve. Ask Ruchi to explain how she approaches Reiki and what participation would involve before deciding to proceed.

There is no single correct experience. You may find the pause meaningful, or simply notice thoughts and sensations as they come and go. You remain free to ask questions, express preferences or pause. Discuss any concerns about touch and personal boundaries in advance rather than assuming what the practice includes.

Preparing for your session

Choose comfortable clothing and let Ruchi know about seating or accessibility needs. If you are preparing a space, ordinary comfortable seating is enough; no incense, ritual or special equipment is required.

Bring one gentle intention, or arrive unsure and talk it through. Ask Ruchi about the session format, duration and fee before arranging a session.

Do I need to believe in Reiki to enquire?+

What if I do not notice a particular sensation?+
