About this practice

Questions about purpose, belonging and connection do not always have quick answers. Spiritual Healing offers a way to explore these questions through your own values and spiritual interests. You might be curious about your inner life, reconsidering what matters to you, or looking for a thoughtful pause during a period of change.

What to expect

The starting point is what spiritual wellbeing means to you. It might involve a sense of connection, an existing tradition, or questions you have not yet found words for. You can discuss these interests with Ruchi and ask how her approach relates to them.

Rather than searching for a hidden cause of life's difficulties, keep the focus on your experience and the choices available to you. A useful question might be, "What would living more closely to my values look like this week?" You do not need to accept an interpretation that does not fit.

Spiritual ideas are matters of tradition and personal belief. The aim is exploration, not certainty about unseen forces or a promised transformation. You can decide which themes feel meaningful and which you would rather leave aside.

Preparing for your session

Consider a question, value or intention you would like to explore. A few personal notes are optional; you do not need to share private experiences before you feel ready.

Tell Ruchi about any practices you prefer not to include. Comfortable seating is welcome, and no ritual preparation is necessary. Ask Ruchi about the session format, duration and fee before arranging a session.
