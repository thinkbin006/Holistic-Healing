About this practice

An image can sometimes invite a question you had not thought to ask. Tarot Card Reading uses symbolic card imagery as a starting point for reflection on your own experience. You might bring a personal choice, a changing priority or a situation you would like to consider from another angle.

What to expect

Ask Ruchi how she uses the cards and how your question will guide the conversation. The imagery and its interpretations offer themes to consider, not scientific information or a fixed account of who you are.

For example, a question about work could become, "What matters to me in the next step I choose?" A symbolic theme might invite you to reflect on patience, boundaries or change. You are free to find it relevant, question it or decide that it does not fit your situation.

A reading cannot guarantee events, uncover another person's thoughts or settle an important decision for you. Keep practical evidence and your own judgement at the centre. When a question needs medical, legal or financial expertise, seek that advice rather than using cards to determine an answer.

Preparing for your session

Bring one open question focused on your own choices. You do not need to know card meanings or have previous experience. A notebook is optional if you would like to record your own reflections.

Keep names and private details about other people out of the initial enquiry. No special ritual or belief is required to ask about the service. Ask Ruchi about the session format, duration and fee before arranging a session.

Before you begin

A little more clarity.

Can Tarot predict what will happen?+

No certain future outcome is offered. Card imagery is used as a prompt to explore your perspective and choices, not as evidence of events yet to happen.

Can a reading tell me what someone else thinks?+

Cards cannot establish another person's private thoughts, intentions or behaviour. A useful question stays with your own experience, boundaries and possible actions.
