About this practice

Mindful movement can start with curiosity about what feels comfortable today, rather than trying to achieve a particular shape. This offering brings together yoga postures, breathing and meditation for wellbeing exploration. Begin with a conversation about suitability so you can decide whether the proposed practice is appropriate for you.

What to expect

Ask Ruchi how movement, breath awareness and meditation would be combined, and which adaptations could accommodate your needs. The aim is not to push through discomfort or compare your movement with someone else's. Resting, changing position and declining a movement are valid choices.

You might explore noticing a comfortable range of movement or attending to natural breathing without forcing it. Any proposed activity should be explained before you try it. Do not assume that a posture or breathing technique is suitable simply because it is described as gentle.

The service name does not establish a medical qualification. Relevant training and the limits of this offering should be clarified with Ruchi. No treatment of injuries, organ improvement or cure is promised, and this practice does not replace medical treatment or rehabilitation.

Preparing for your session

Discuss suitability with Ruchi and your treating professional where needed, particularly if you have an injury, a health condition, are pregnant or are recovering from treatment.

Wear clothing that allows comfortable movement and ask what seating or equipment would be appropriate; floor sitting is not required. Stop if a practice causes pain, dizziness or distress. Ask Ruchi about the session format, duration and fee before arranging a session.

Before you begin

A little more clarity.

Do I need to be flexible or able to sit on the floor?+

No level of flexibility is a prerequisite for an enquiry. Discuss your movement and seating needs with Ruchi before agreeing on a suitable practice; floor sitting should not be assumed.

What if I have an injury or health condition?+

Discuss suitability with your treating professional and Ruchi before taking part. Ask about relevant training and possible adaptations, and continue any professional care already recommended.
